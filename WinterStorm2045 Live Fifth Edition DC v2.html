NATO STO SAC-219 // High North Scenarios for Wargaming & Analysis

WinterStorm2045

Stress-testing NATO's governance capacity across the grey zone.

Today NATO faces a growing set of challenges across the Arctic High North. Blue Team analysts are operating in the grey zone — pressure from Russia and China that stays deliberately below the threshold of open conflict — and need to explore scenarios they haven't seen yet, not just refight familiar ones. WinterStorm2045 exists to stress-test both: the known scenarios and the unknowns. Every session tracks a single live number, the NATO Capacity Gap (Cap Gap — the deficit between allied governance capacity and what the threat actually requires), as it responds to each move you and the adversary make.

Technical — Emerging & Disruptive Technology
AI-driven disinformation, dual-use biosecurity, autonomous systems, digital-twin logistics, and PNT jurisdiction gaps — the governance questions a new capacity raises before doctrine catches up to it.
Science — Arctic Environmental Reality
Acoustic and sea-ice conditions, solar-cycle/ionospheric interference, permafrost and storm-surge trends, and a live-or-synthetic temperature & sea-level feed — the physical environment that independently shapes what governance can actually deliver, not merely what it intends.

Both are first-class, mathematically-modeled inputs to the same instrument — not narrative color. See Governance Analytics for the live E-IAIG-HT figures, and the NATO Cap Gap tab's sixth tile for how the science layer is moving the number right now.

Per Section VI: classical Monte Carlo converges as O(1/√N) — 4× the samples to halve the error — while QAE estimates the same outcome probability using Grover's operator and phase estimation in roughly √N queries; the spec's own example is 2,000 classical samples ≈ 45 QAE queries for comparable accuracy. This panel reuses the exact Monte Carlo engine and inputs above and compares three estimators of P(Win-Win) / P(Critical Breach) side by side.
SCOPE OF THIS PANEL — There is no classical substitute for genuine quadratic quantum speedup — Grover-based amplitude estimation needs an actual quantum circuit. What runs here is the closest honest classical analog: stratified sampling on the dominant source of variance (Red's first-move tag), which — like QAE — squeezes more accuracy out of a small sample by removing a known source of noise rather than averaging over it. It demonstrates the "fewer samples, comparable accuracy" shape of the claim; it does not achieve true quadratic scaling.

Pareto Dominance // 5-Domain Cap Gap
📊 PARETO — "Where should NATO intervene first?"
What this panel does
A genuine two-objective Pareto dominance calculation over the 5 live Cap Gap domains — not a re-sorted bar chart. Severity is each domain's current gap score; Trajectory is its net recent movement from the actual Gap Event Log (Red exploits widen it, Blue closes narrow it). A domain sits on the Priority Frontier if no other domain is at least as severe and at least as fast-worsening. Domains off the frontier aren't unimportant — they're dominated: whatever justifies intervening in the domain that dominates them already covers their case too.
HOW THIS DIFFERS FROM CAP GAP / PSTOA — The Cap Gap Composite tab shows current severity only, one dimension. PSTOA's Weight Sensitivity note flags only the single largest one-off impact this session. This is the only panel doing real two-objective dominance filtering — a domain with moderate severity but the fastest recent trend can dominate one with a higher score but a stable or improving trend, which a simple ranked list would miss.
