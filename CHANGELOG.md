# Changelog

All notable changes to the customer-facing WinterStorm2045 build.

## Fifth Edition DC v2

- Build renamed **Fifth Edition DC v2**. The login page, page title, header and session export carry the new label.
- Terminology: the gap metric is the **NATO Capacity Gap** throughout, matching what it measures (institutional governance capacity, not military capability). The Workbench tab is **Capacity Cards**. "Disruptive Capabilities" is kept where it names the SAS-219 R4 subgroup.
- Agentic AI Guide extended across the instrument, with tab-by-tab orientation.

## Fifth Edition v1

- **E-IAIG-HT Fifth Revised Edition, Rev. 1 mathematics:** one canonical g-GWC equation; the recomputed actor baseline; Φ = G_eff / G_inst as the single environmental factor; EI drag on environment-sensitive domains only; EI from 24-hour means against day-interpolated NOAA 1991–2020 normals and the non-tidal water-level residual; the Escalation Composite on the institutional Capacity Gap with round-half-up scoring; geometric EOC interpolation.
- **Verification:** test vectors TV-1 to TV-13 run on every page load, with results on the login page and in Governance Analytics.
- **Agentic AI Guide:** landing-page orientation, scenario build, Live Theatre introduction, narration of every Blue and Red move with measured effects, Environments and White Cell prompts, After-Action Report read-out with next-step recommendations, and Read aloud.
- **End of game:** confirmation on End Game; Start Another Game in the header, guide and After-Action Report; Log Out of Simulation; the After-Action Report opens after the final Red move has been read.
- "Quantum Optimizer" relabelled "QUBO Optimizer".

## v26

- **White Cell:** semi-rigid adjudication for the Contested Table and Arbitration Track, overrides with logged reasons, advisory AI drafts, and a calibration signal.
- **Scenario 12, Greenland Slope Collapse (Methane Hydrate Event, 2045)** with a sealed cause and cause-dependent scoring.
- **Decision-Pressure Field** as the default Live Theatre map.
